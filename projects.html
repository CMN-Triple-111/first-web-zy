Core Initiatives

Key Projects & Field Initiatives

Each initiative is tailored to empower smallholders, restore land fertility, and foster youth-driven agricultural sovereignty.

Bio-Inputs & Supply

Sustainable Bio-Fertilizer Distribution

Distributing affordable, non-toxic Bokashi & Benkaol bio-fertilizer packages to small-scale farmers in rural Chongwe and Lusaka Province, reducing input costs by up to 50%.

Education & Seed Heritage

Seed Heritage & Capacity Workshops

Interactive village-level exhibitions and workshops promoting indigenous seeds, soil microbial biology, organic composting, and drought-tolerant cropping methods.

Youth Agribusiness

Youth in Agriculture Program

Mentoring unemployed rural and peri-urban youth to become certified agro-ecological producers, bio-fertilizer technicians, and agricultural entrepreneurs.

Food Security

Community Demonstration Gardens

Co-creating community vegetable plots that double as living laboratories and high-nutrient food sources for vulnerable families and school feeding programs.

Ecology & Forestry

Agroforestry & Soil Regeneration

Integrating nitrogen-fixing indigenous trees, windbreaks, and cover cropping into smallholder fields to combat land degradation and halt deforestation.

Water & Health

Clean Water Access & Protection

Protecting farming village groundwater and local streams from agrochemical runoff, while facilitating sustainable irrigation points for family plots.
